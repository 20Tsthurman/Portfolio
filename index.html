Digital Bloom Socials Portal

A two-sided business platform I designed and built for a local social media and videography agency, now running its day-to-day operations. The owner portal handles scheduling, content planning, time tracking, invoicing, and bookkeeping; clients log in to review each month's content before it's released.

Key features

  • Google Calendar sync with clear ownership: shoots originate in the portal and push to Google, while personal events sync back as read-only busy blocks, so nothing is entered twice
  • Monthly content calendar with draft → client review → release cycles
  • Client retainer budgets tracked against logged hours
  • Sole-proprietor bookkeeping: Schedule C profit estimate, tax set-aside, mileage deductions, one-click CPA export package
  • Stripe-connected invoicing
